Texas Railroad Commission — Gas Purchase & Distributed Energy Resource Filings
The Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC) regulates natural gas operations, including gas purchases and distributed energy resources (DERs), through its Oil & Gas Division and Gas Services programs.

Gas Purchase Filings
For gas purchases, the RRC’s Oil & Gas Division requires certain operators to file Form P‑5 (Organization Report) and related reports under 16 Texas Administrative Code (TAC) Chapter 3. This applies if your organization:

Operates as a gas and/or liquids company.

Owns or operates master metered properties.

Engages in gas transportation, storage, or processing.

Operates a gas distribution system.
Key requirements:

Maintain a current Form P‑5 with updated officers, agents, and addresses.

File Statewide Rule 58 (Certificate of Compliance and Transportation Authority; Operator Reports) if transporting gas.

Pay applicable filing fees:

$300 for initial Form P‑5.

Annual renewal based on activities (up to $1,350).

$225 if the only activity is operating one or more gas distribution systems and master metered properties

Distributed Energy Resource (DER) Filings
The Gas Services division oversees alternative fuels, gas services, and pipeline safety, including DERs such as:

Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) stations.

Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) facilities.

Propane distribution systems.

Other alternative fuel infrastructure.

DER filings typically involve:

Form P‑5 for any DER operator within RRC jurisdiction.

Compliance with 16 TAC Chapter 7 (Gas Services) and Chapter 13 (CNG) or Chapter 14 (LNG) rules.

Reporting requirements for alternative fuel operations, including safety, environmental, and operational data.

Filing fees per Form P‑5 rules, plus any DER-specific surcharges under Statewide Rule 78

Where to File
Online filing portal: The RRC provides an online filing system for Forms P‑5 and related reports.

Forms & rules: Access via the RRC’s Oil & Gas Division and Gas Services pages.

Contact: Use the RRC’s online filing system or contact the Oil & Gas Division for guidance.

Tip: If you operate both gas purchases and DERs, ensure your Form P‑5 covers all activities, and file all applicable rules and fees to avoid compliance penalties.

For the most current forms, rules, and fee schedules, visit the RRC’s official site and check the Current Rules section for 16 TAC Chapter 3 and Chapter 7.
